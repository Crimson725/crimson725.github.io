Zhixin Zeng 曾智鑫

Independent Researcher

I work on machine learning and reinforcement learning. My current research is on agents for video editing: giving an agent the checks, feedback, and editable history that coding agents already get from compilers and version control. Earlier, I worked on inverse reinforcement learning for urban safety perception and on fake news detection. I hold an M.S. in Computer Science from Northeastern University and a B.S. from Sichuan University.
